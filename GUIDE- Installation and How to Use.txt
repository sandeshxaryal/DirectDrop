How to install:
1. Follow step 1 and install the .NET8 sdk from the folder or download from official site. (ignore if already installed)
2. Open step 2 folder and run "Install DirectDrop.bat"

How to use:
1) Open DirectDrop in your PC.
2) Allow permissions as asked.
3) Connect to the Wi-Fi network by scanning the qr code from your android/iPhone/other device.
4) Again scan qr code from your android/iPhone/other device to open a link in your browser from where you can transfer files.

Enjoy!
